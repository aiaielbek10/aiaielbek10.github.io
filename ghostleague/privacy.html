Ghost League Soccer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Ghost League Soccer ("the game") is a single-player soccer game. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (team name, manager name, players, coins, gems, league results and settings) is saved only on your device. The age range you select on the first screen is stored on your device and is used only to show appropriate content.

Google Play services (optional)

  • Google Play Games cloud save — if you sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress is stored in your own Google Play Games "Saved Games" so you can continue on another phone. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • In-app purchases — purchases (for example the Season Pass) are processed by Google Play. The game never sees your card or payment details.

What we do not do

The game does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, does not collect your contacts, location, photos or other personal data, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your device. You can delete cloud saves in the Google Play Games app, or contact us.
