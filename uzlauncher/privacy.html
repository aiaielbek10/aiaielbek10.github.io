Uz Launcher — Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-10-25

Uz Launcher — Android telefon uchun bosh ekran (launcher). U ilovalarni ko'rsatadi, foydalanuvchi xohlagan ilovalarni bosh ekrandan yashiradi va ularni maxfiy kod bilan ochiladigan kalkulyator orqali ko'rsatadi. Ilovada internet ruxsati yo'q, shuning uchun hech qanday ma'lumot telefondan tashqariga yuborilmaydi.

Qanday ma'lumotlar ishlatiladi

  • O'rnatilgan ilovalar ro'yxati — bosh ekran va ilovalar menyusida ilovalarni ko'rsatish uchun.
  • Bildirishnomalar (ixtiyoriy, foydalanuvchi o'zi ruxsat beradi) — ilova belgisida yangi xabarlar sonini ko'rsatish va yashirilgan ilovalarning bildirishnomalarini ekrandan olib tashlash uchun. Bildirishnomalar matni o'qilmaydi va saqlanmaydi, faqat ularning soni hisoblanadi.
  • Rasm (ixtiyoriy) — foydalanuvchi ilova belgisini o'zgartirish uchun galereyadan rasm tanlasa, o'sha rasmning kichik nusxasi faqat ilova ichida saqlanadi.
  • Maxfiy kod — telefonda faqat shifrlangan (SHA-256 xesh) ko'rinishda saqlanadi.

Ma'lumotlarni saqlash va ulashish

Barcha sozlamalar faqat qurilmada saqlanadi. Ma'lumotlar yig'ilmaydi, serverga yuborilmaydi, sotilmaydi va uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi. Ilovada reklama va analitika yo'q. Ilovani o'chirsangiz, barcha sozlamalar ham o'chadi.

Uz Launcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Uz Launcher is a home-screen launcher for Android. It shows your apps, lets you hide chosen apps from the home screen and opens them through a calculator protected by a secret code. The app has no internet permission, so no data ever leaves your phone.

  • Installed apps list — used only to display apps on the home screen and in the app drawer.
  • Notifications (optional, granted by the user) — used only to show unread counts on app icons and to dismiss notifications of hidden apps. Notification content is not read or stored.
  • Images (optional) — if you pick a picture as a custom icon, a small copy is stored inside the app only.
  • Secret code — stored on the device only as a SHA-256 hash.

No data is collected, transmitted, sold or shared. No ads, no analytics. Uninstalling the app removes all its data.
