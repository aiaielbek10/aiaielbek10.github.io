Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 5-noyabr. Ishlab chiquvchi: Elbek O'rinboyev (lgcompyuter@gmail.com).

Qanday ma'lumotlar yig'iladi

  • Telefon raqam — hisobga kirish va sizni kontaktlaringiz topishi uchun.
  • Ism, familiya, foydalanuvchi nomi, bio, profil rasmi — o'zingiz kiritganlar; suhbatdoshlaringizga ko'rinadi.
  • Xabarlar, rasmlar, ovozli va video xabarlar, fayllar, so'rovnomalar, hikoyalar — yetkazib berish va boshqa qurilmalaringizda ko'rsatish uchun serverda saqlanadi.
  • Kontaktlar — faqat "Telefon kontaktlarini sinxronlash" tugmasini bossangiz: raqam va ismlar kim ELCHI'da ekanini aniqlash uchun serverga yuboriladi.
  • Oxirgi faollik vaqti — maxfiylik sozlamangizga qarab boshqalarga ko'rsatiladi yoki yashiriladi.
  • Mikrofon va kamera — faqat ovozli xabar, video xabar, rasm va qo'ng'iroq paytida, sizning ruxsatingiz bilan. Qo'ng'iroqlarda ovoz va video qurilmadan qurilmaga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri uzatiladi va serverda saqlanmaydi.

Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Ma'lumotlar Supabase xizmati serverlarida (Yevropa Ittifoqi, Frankfurt) saqlanadi, uzatishda shifrlanadi (HTTPS/TLS). Ma'lumotlaringiz sotilmaydi, reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi va uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi.

Sizning huquqlaringiz

  • Xabarlaringizni o'chirishingiz, guruh va kanallardan chiqishingiz, foydalanuvchilarni bloklashingiz mumkin.
  • Hisobingizni va unga tegishli barcha ma'lumotlarni butunlay o'chirish uchun lgcompyuter@gmail.com manziliga hisob raqamingiz bilan yozing — 30 kun ichida o'chiriladi.

ELCHI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026. Developer: Elbek O'rinboyev (lgcompyuter@gmail.com).

Data we collect

  • Phone number — to sign in and let your contacts find you.
  • Name, username, bio, profile photo — entered by you; visible to people you chat with.
  • Messages, photos, voice and video messages, files, polls, stories — stored on the server to deliver them and show them on your other devices.
  • Contacts — only if you tap "Sync phone contacts": numbers and names are sent to find which contacts use ELCHI.
  • Last seen time — shown or hidden according to your privacy setting.
  • Microphone and camera — only for voice/video messages, photos and calls, with your permission. Call audio and video go directly between devices and are not stored on the server.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on Supabase servers (EU, Frankfurt) and encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Your rights

You can delete messages, leave groups and channels, and block users. To delete your account and all its data, email lgcompyuter@gmail.com from or with your account phone number; deletion is completed within 30 days.

Children

ELCHI is not intended for children under 13.
