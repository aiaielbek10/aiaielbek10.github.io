Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Uzbek Racing (package name: com.uzbekracing.game) handles information.

1. Information we collect

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, phone number or email. Game progress (coins, unlocked cars, licence plate, music settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. If you add your own music, a copy of the selected audio file is stored only in the game's folder on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect: the advertising ID, device and operating system information, IP address and approximate location, and ad interactions (views, clicks). Google processes this data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads). In regions such as the EEA, a consent form is shown when the game is first opened.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page.
