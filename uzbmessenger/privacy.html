UZB Messenger — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 10-noyabr. Ishlab chiquvchi: Elbek O'rinboyev (lgcompyuter@gmail.com).

UZB Messenger — Telegram uchun o'zbek tilidagi norasmiy mijoz ilova. U ochiq Telegram API orqali ishlaydi va Telegram'ning rasmiy ilovasi emas. Ilovaning o'z serveri yo'q: ishlab chiquvchi sizning xabarlaringiz yoki shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni ko'rmaydi, yig'maydi va saqlamaydi.

Qanday ma'lumotlar ishlatiladi

  • Telefon raqam, tasdiqlash kodi va ikki bosqichli parol — faqat Telegram hisobingizga kirish uchun to'g'ridan-to'g'ri Telegram serverlariga yuboriladi.
  • Xabarlar, rasmlar, video, ovozli va dumaloq video-xabarlar, fayllar, stikerlar, hikoyalar — yuborish va qabul qilish uchun Telegram serverlariga uzatiladi; tezroq ko'rsatish uchun qurilmangiz xotirasida keshlanadi.
  • Kontaktlar — faqat siz ruxsat bersangiz va kontaktlarni import qilsangiz: kimlar Telegram'da ekanini aniqlash uchun Telegram'ga yuboriladi.
  • Mikrofon va kamera — faqat ovozli xabar, dumaloq video, rasm va QR kodni skanerlash paytida, sizning ruxsatingiz bilan.
  • Sozlamalar (til, mavzu, jildlar va h.k.) — faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi.

Uchinchi tomonlar

Barcha ma'lumotlar faqat Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc.) serverlari bilan almashinadi va ularga Telegram maxfiylik siyosati amal qiladi. Ilovada reklama, analitika yoki kuzatuv vositalari yo'q. Ma'lumotlaringiz sotilmaydi va boshqa hech kimga berilmaydi. Uzatish shifrlangan (MTProto).

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

  • Ilovadan chiqish (Sozlamalar → Chiqish) yoki ilovani o'chirish qurilmadagi barcha kesh va sozlamalarni o'chiradi.
  • Xabarlaringizni ilova ichida o'chirishingiz mumkin. Telegram hisobingizni butunlay o'chirish: my.telegram.org/delete.
  • Savollar uchun: lgcompyuter@gmail.com.

UZB Messenger — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026. Developer: Elbek O'rinboyev (lgcompyuter@gmail.com).

UZB Messenger is an unofficial Uzbek-language client for Telegram. It works through the public Telegram API and is not an official Telegram app. The app has no server of its own: the developer does not see, collect or store your messages or personal data.

Data the app uses

  • Phone number, login code and two-step verification password — sent directly to Telegram servers only to sign in to your Telegram account.
  • Messages, photos, videos, voice and video messages, files, stickers, stories — sent to and received from Telegram servers; cached on your device for faster display.
  • Contacts — only if you grant permission and import them: sent to Telegram to find which contacts use Telegram.
  • Microphone and camera — only while recording voice/video messages, taking photos or scanning QR codes, with your permission.
  • Settings (language, theme, folders, etc.) — stored only on your device.

Third parties

All data is exchanged only with Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc.) servers and is governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy. The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone else. Transfer is encrypted (MTProto).

Data deletion

Logging out (Settings → Log out) or uninstalling the app removes all cached data and settings from your device. You can delete messages inside the app. To delete your Telegram account entirely: my.telegram.org/delete. Questions: lgcompyuter@gmail.com.

Children

UZB Messenger is not intended for children under 13.
